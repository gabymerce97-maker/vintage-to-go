# Vintage to Go

A free vintage photo booth that runs in your browser.

**Try it:** https://gabymerce97-maker.github.io/vintage-to-go/

1. **The booth**: a 3-2-1 countdown takes 3, 4 or 6 poses with your front camera (or upload your own photos).
2. **The strip**: your photos slide out of the slot and develop.
3. **Final touches**: 6 canvas sizes (Portrait, Story, Square, Postcard, Landscape, Strip only), 4 layouts, 7 frames, 47 filters, 14 papers, stickers, captions and an orange date stamp. Save as PNG.

Everything happens on your device: photos are never uploaded anywhere.

**Camera tips**
- Open the link in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android) and tap Allow when asked.
- Instagram, TikTok and other in-app browsers can block the camera. Use “Open in browser” from the app's menu.
